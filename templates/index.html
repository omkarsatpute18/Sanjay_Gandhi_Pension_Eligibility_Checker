Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar Pension Yojana is a Maharashtra state scheme providing monthly financial assistance to destitute and economically vulnerable citizens.

Widows, orphans, disabled persons, and other destitute individuals falling below the specified income limit and meeting residency criteria are eligible.
